# Choose Your Path

Most people start by downloading the **DevKit**, a copy of the DuploCloud platform that runs on your own machine. You can build and test agents there before anything touches your cloud. If you'd rather not start locally, you can begin in the DuploCloud Console or the Agent Marketplace.

## Where to start

| Start here | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **DevKit** (recommended) | Run the whole platform on your laptop, connect a cloud account, and build and test your own agents. First run gets you a trial license. See [Install the DevKit](install-the-devkit.md). |
| [**DuploCloud Console**](https://console.duplocloud.com) | Your DuploCloud account: request and manage licenses, buy paid agents, and manage your team. |
| [**Agent Marketplace**](https://marketplace.duplocloud.com) | Browse the agents DuploCloud publishes, both included and paid, before you install anything. You install agents from inside your portal. |

## Free and paid

A **free** license gets you a trial for building locally in the DevKit and one environment in your own cloud. Free licenses have limits on users, workspaces, providers and agents. A **paid** or customer license lifts those limits. The [License](licensing.md) page explains how licenses work and where to see your limits.

## The journey

Each step links to the page that covers it.

| # | Step | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Install the DevKit](install-the-devkit.md) | Run the platform locally and sign in. |
| 2 | [Get a license](licensing.md) | The DevKit requests a trial license for you on first run. Paid licenses come from DuploCloud. |
| 3 | Build agents | Build and test agents in the DevKit against your own cloud accounts. See [Install the DevKit](install-the-devkit.md#where-to-go-next). |
| 4 | [Deploy your portal](installation/README.md) | Put your own DuploCloud portal into your cloud account. |
| 5 | [Install agents](marketplace.md) or [deploy your own](deploy-your-own-agents.md) | Add agents from the marketplace, or push the agents you built to your portal. |

{% hint style="info" %}
In some cases DuploCloud may deploy the portal for you. Talk to your account team if you'd like to discuss it.
{% endhint %}
