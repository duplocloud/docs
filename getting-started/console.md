# DuploCloud Console

The [DuploCloud Console](https://console.duplocloud.com) is your DuploCloud account. It sits outside your portal and DevKit. You use it to:

* Get and renew licenses. See [Licensing](licensing.md).
* Create and manage environments. See [Create an Environment](create-an-environment.md).
* Buy paid agents and manage purchases. See [Agent Marketplace and Buying Agents](marketplace.md).
* Manage the people in your company.
